Ridgeline

Search-and-rescue drones that keep working when the mountain cuts the signal.

28.17° N 85.31° E Langtang range from Dhunche, Rasuwa Scroll

Rasuwa district, Nepal

26 August 2026

  1. 08:37

    The mountain gives way

    Rock and ice break off the north face of Langtang Lirung, about 5,200 m up.

  2. Minutes later

    The river is dammed, then released

    The debris blocks the Lhende Khola. When it breaks, the surge hits Gyirong Port on the border and runs south into Nepal.

  3. Within 30 minutes

    The valley floods

    The Bhote Koshi and the Trishuli rise by up to 9 m through Rasuwa and Nuwakot.

  4. Before 09:00

    The signal goes

    Phone towers along the valley are destroyed. Trekkers, villages and rescuers lose contact.

  5. By 5 October

    1,455people killed in Nepal

    5,285 still missing when the search ended.
