Not just Nepal

Wherever the network fails,
the swarm becomes one.

01 · Defence

Jammed, not blind

Jamming cuts drones off from their operator. A swarm that shares one memory routes around the jammed zone and keeps the picture whole.

02 · Drone shows

The show goes on

Hundreds of drones, one controller. When lights drop out, their neighbours regrow the shape, the same self-repair our drones compute.

03 · Sport

Live from the mountain stage

Tour de France pictures reach TV through relay helicopters and planes circling overhead. A drone chain over the col could carry the feed instead.

04 · Blackouts

The first 72 hours

The EU, the UK and France tell households to be ready to cope alone for 72 hours. When towers lose power, drones can carry calls for help until crews arrive.
