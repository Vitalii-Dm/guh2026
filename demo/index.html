      The gecko network, running on the drones

      A neural cellular automaton trained like the self-repairing gecko, on our V formation. Each tinted patch is computed by the drone on its badge. A cell only sees its 8 neighbours, so drones swap the edge cells of their patches by radio: white borders, brighter while they're busy rebuilding. Red dashed borders: that neighbour is out of reach, so the drone works from the last copy it got. Fail a drone and its neighbours take over its patch and regrow it. Click a drone to see its copy.
